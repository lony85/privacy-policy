Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Natiovera ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. Natiovera does not require you to create an account and does not directly ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

Natiovera does not directly collect personal information from users. The application uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand general application usage and improve the app.

Analytics information may include:

  • App usage and interaction information
  • Screens or features viewed
  • Device and app-related information
  • Approximate geographic information
  • Session and diagnostic information
  • Application performance and error information

2. How We Use Information

Analytics information is used to understand how Natiovera is used and to improve the application.

  • Understand application usage
  • Improve application performance
  • Identify technical issues
  • Improve existing features
  • Develop and improve future features
  • Understand general usage trends

3. Third-Party Services

Natiovera uses third-party services to provide analytics and publicly available country and development information.

Google Analytics for Firebase

Natiovera uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect analytics information about how the application is used.

Google may process information collected through its services according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

World Bank API

Natiovera retrieves publicly available economic, demographic, and development data from the World Bank API.

This service is used to provide country and development indicators within the application.

REST Countries API

Natiovera may retrieve publicly available country information, including country names, flags, capitals, currencies, languages, and area information through the REST Countries API.

4. Data Sharing

Natiovera does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Analytics information may be processed by Google as part of the Google Analytics for Firebase service and is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the use of Natiovera. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Natiovera does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Natiovera's privacy practices, you can contact us through the contact information provided with the Natiovera application or its associated website.
