Privacy Policy for Elixino

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

Elixino ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Elixino mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Elixino may collect certain information automatically through Google Analytics for Firebase.

This information may include:

  • App usage and interaction data
  • Events and actions performed within the app
  • Device and technical information
  • Approximate geographic information
  • App version and operating system information
  • Information about how users interact with app features

Google Analytics may automatically collect certain events and user properties as part of its standard functionality.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment information, contacts, photos, or precise location through Elixino.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through analytics is used to:

  • Understand how users interact with Elixino
  • Improve the application's features and user experience
  • Identify technical issues and improve app performance
  • Measure application usage and engagement
  • Understand which features and content are most useful

We do not use analytics information to identify individual users personally.

3. Google Analytics for Firebase

Elixino uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google LLC.

Google Analytics helps us understand how users use the application by collecting usage and behavioral information, including events and certain user properties.

Google may process this information according to its own privacy practices.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Third-Party APIs

Elixino uses third-party APIs to retrieve cocktail recipes, ingredients, and related information.

When you search for or request cocktail information, your request may be transmitted to the relevant API service in order to provide the requested content.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal information.

Analytics and technical information may be processed by third-party service providers, including Google Analytics for Firebase, for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Elixino does not maintain a personal user account system or intentionally store sensitive personal information on our own servers.

Analytics data may be retained and processed by Google according to Google's applicable policies and retention settings.

Some application preferences or saved content may be stored locally on the user's device.

7. Advertising

Elixino does not currently use advertising services to display advertisements in the application.

Google Analytics is used for application analytics and measurement.

8. Children's Privacy

Elixino is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Elixino.

Network communication with supported services is performed using secure connections where supported.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Choices

Depending on the device, operating system, and applicable Google services, users may have options to control certain analytics or advertising-related data collection.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Elixino, third-party services, or applicable requirements.

The "Last Updated" date will be updated whenever material changes are made.
